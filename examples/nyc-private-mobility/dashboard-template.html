Eligibility comes first

    Hub distance is straight-line from the zone centroid in UTM 18N. It is not a driving distance or travel time.

    How the score is built

    Demand, coverage gap and fleet saturation are normalized against the complete eligible pool, not the currently visible shortlist. Market context combines private scores, amenities and competition.

    Snapshot provenance

    Three live services. Seeded demo rows.

    These pinned snapshots were captured through read-only connector sessions. PostGIS and BigQuery also used restricted readers; the MotherDuck token is writable outside its connector session.
